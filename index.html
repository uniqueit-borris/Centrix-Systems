Standard Tier Definitions

P1
Critical

Complete service outage, data loss risk, or security breach. All business operations halted. Immediate all-hands response required.

P2
High

Major functionality impaired, significant user group affected, or partial service outage. No acceptable workaround available.

P3
Medium

Non-critical functionality impaired with a workaround available. Limited number of users affected. Normal business operations continue.

P4
Low

Minor cosmetic issues, feature requests, how-to questions, or general information requests. No business impact.
